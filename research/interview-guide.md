# Field Interview Guide

## Purpose

Understand how jewellers and karigars currently manage outsourced job-work, material movement, reconciliation and settlement. Validate the problem before finalising product requirements.

Do not pitch the solution at the beginning. Ask about actual workflows first.

## Questions for jewellers

1. Do you outsource manufacturing, polishing, repair or finishing to independent karigars?
2. How many active karigars do you typically work with?
3. How are job orders created and communicated?
4. How do you record the material and weight issued?
5. How does the karigar acknowledge receipt?
6. How are purity, process allowance and wastage agreed?
7. How are partial returns recorded?
8. How do you reconcile finished jewellery, scrap and remaining material?
9. What happens when your records and the karigar's records differ?
10. How do you track job deadlines and pending work?
11. How are making charges and settlements recorded?
12. Which tools do you currently use: registers, spreadsheets, WhatsApp or software?
13. What works well in the current process?
14. Which step takes the most time or causes the most confusion?
15. Can you describe a recent anonymised example of a reconciliation challenge?
16. Would a shared record be useful? What concerns would you have?
17. What would make a karigar willing or reluctant to use a digital workflow?
18. Who would decide whether to adopt or pay for such a system?

## Questions for karigars

1. How do you receive job instructions?
2. How do you confirm material received?
3. How do you record work completed and material returned?
4. How do you document scrap, residual material or process allowance?
5. What happens when a jeweller questions a return or weight?
6. What tools or phone access do you currently use for work records?
7. What would make a shared digital record useful or inconvenient?
8. What information should only you be able to see or edit?

## Research ethics

- Ask permission before taking notes, photos or recordings.
- Do not collect customer names, private transaction details or confidential business records.
- Use anonymised examples.
- Do not present opinions as representative of the entire industry.
- Record the date, stakeholder type and consent status for each interview.
