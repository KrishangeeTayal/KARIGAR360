# Solution Overview

## KARIGAR360

KARIGAR360 is a proposed two-sided job-work management and gold-accountability platform connecting jewellers and independent karigars through a shared digital workflow.

## Stakeholders

### Jeweller
- Create and assign job orders.
- Record gold/material issued and agreed terms.
- Monitor acknowledgements, progress and returns.
- Review discrepancies and settlement statements.

### Karigar
- View assigned jobs.
- Acknowledge material received.
- Update production progress.
- Record partial and final returns.
- Attach supporting evidence.
- Review, approve or dispute reconciliation proposals.

## Core capabilities

1. Digital job ledger.
2. Material issue and acknowledgement.
3. Production and deadline tracking.
4. Partial and final return recording.
5. Reconciliation against mutually agreed rules.
6. Evidence-linked discrepancy review.
7. Settlement statement and dual approval.

## Guiding principles

- Shared visibility for both counterparties.
- Clear provenance for every material movement.
- AI assists data capture; it does not decide liability.
- Deterministic arithmetic for reconciliation.
- Disputes remain reviewable by both parties.
- Corrections remain traceable.
- Human approval for settlement and closure.

## Illustrative reconciliation

A hypothetical 100 g issue could be represented as 98.5 g finished material, 0.8 g agreed process allowance and 0.7 g recoverable material, totalling 100 g accounted.

This is only an illustration. It is not an industry-standard allowance, actual transaction or customer result.
