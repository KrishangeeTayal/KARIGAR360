# Problem Statement — PS 2.2 Jeweltech

## Context

Jewellers may engage independent karigars for jewellery manufacturing, polishing, repair and finishing. During job-work, material moves between parties and multiple operational events need to be recorded.

## Problem

The records for issuance, acknowledgement, production progress, partial returns, final returns, reconciliation and settlement may be fragmented across registers, messages, verbal confirmations and weighing slips.

This can make it difficult for both parties to access a consistent, shared history of responsibility across the job lifecycle.

## Problem framing

The challenge is not only to track gold. It is to establish a reliable shared record of what was issued, acknowledged, processed, returned and mutually settled.

## Design objectives

- Give each job a traceable record.
- Capture material transfers and acknowledgement by both parties.
- Record progress and partial/final returns.
- Reconcile material using agreed rules.
- Attach evidence to relevant events.
- Allow both parties to review and dispute differences.
- Require human approval before settlement and job closure.

## Validation status

These are the problem assumptions informing the proposed concept. They must be validated through field conversations with jewellers and karigars. This document does not claim completed interviews or measured industry-wide prevalence.
